Cheyenne Children's Museum aims to launch youth coding program
CHEYENNE – The Cheyenne Children's Museum has announced a proposed new program, Outside the Bot, that aims to introduce the community's younger youth to foundational coding concepts through engaging, hands-on activities.
Participants will develop introductory skills in algorithms, logic, interaction and problem-solving in a beginner-friendly environment by establishing a foundation before entering the existing 4-H robotics program. Outside the Bot will help build confidence, reduce barriers to participation and create a more accessible pathway into STEM education, according to the museum's October newsletter.
