Cowgirls tennis picks up 7 wins to open Bobcat Invite
University of Wyoming tennis opened the Bobcat Invite with seven wins Friday in Bozeman, Montana.
The three-day event features mock duals against Idaho State, Montana State and Weber State. Teams receive one point for each doubles and singles match win, with the team with the most points at the end taking home the title.
The Cowgirls won a pair of doubles matches and five singles contests against Idaho State. In doubles, the teams of Eugenia Ceinos and Zlata Nesterova won, as did the tandem of Antonella Taco and Ruby Young. Ceinos and Nesterova won 6-2, while Taco and Young took a 6-4 victory.
In singles, Ceinos won in straight sets, 6-3 and 7-5. Dilnaz Mashabayeva made quick work of her opponent, 6-1, 6-3. Nesterova also won in straight sets, 6-3 and 6-3. Taco and Young won in straight sets and pulled off second-set tiebreakers. Taco won 6-2 and 7-6 (8-6), while Young was victorious 6-4 and 7-6 (7-3).
UW faces Montana State at 9 a.m. Saturday.
