## 1. Debt paperwork: a malformed estimate, not a $10 billion authorization

### What the sources say

The [unsigned application resolution, PDF pages 1–2](https://cheyenne.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?view_id=5&clip_id=1099&meta_id=148538#page=1)
authorizes applying for **not more than $20,000,000** from the Wyoming Drinking
Water State Revolving Fund for lead-service-line investigation and replacement.
It describes Water Enterprise Fund revenues as the repayment source.

Its page 2 says:

> the annual principal and interest payment on the proposed loan will range
> from approximately $750,000.00 to $1,000,0000.00 based on principal forgiveness
> amount awarded

That is an **estimated payment range in a WHEREAS clause**. It is not the earlier
supplied “Applicant debt service shall not exceed…” quotation, and it is not an
operative $10 billion borrowing authority. The malformed string is preserved
literally here; no intended replacement amount has been invented.

The [June 22 adoption-packet attachment, page 2](https://cheyenne.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?view_id=5&clip_id=1103&meta_id=148947#page=2)
was freshly captured as well. It is byte-identical to the earlier attachment:
this is a reused document, not two independent confirmations of its accuracy.
Both copies leave adoption/signature fields blank.

The [June 22 minutes, page 4](https://cheyenne.granicus.com/DocumentViewer.php?file=cheyenne_964bdd5707a3db4918c35f6e987ac155.pdf&view=1#page=4)
record Esquibel moving, White seconding, adoption of the application resolution
as **6544**, with all governing-body members **present** voting yes. The date
comes from the minutes' page 1, not the archive index, which labels that meeting
June 23. Attendance also includes remote arrivals/departures; no separate named
roll-call rows or precise attendance-at-vote times were inferred.

### Financial interpretation

- **Application cap:** $20 million, not proof that a $20 million loan closed or
  that the City received/spent that amount.
- **Estimated origination fee:** 0.5% × $20 million = $100,000, consistent with
  the draft's stated estimate—not evidence of an actual fee payment.
- **Draft financing assumptions:** zero interest and up to 25% principal
  forgiveness. Actual award and executed terms have not been examined; do not
  treat maximum possible forgiveness as guaranteed.
- **Repayment estimate:** visibly malformed and worth obtaining in certified
  form. The unsigned draft plus adoption minutes do not prove whether a signed
  copy retained or corrected it, or determine its legal effect.
- The draft's statements about EPA rules/timelines have not been independently
  validated as current federal legal requirements.

**Accountability question:** what repayment schedule, fee, forgiveness and
borrowing obligation did the City/BOPU actually execute, and does the certified
resolution accurately describe them?

## 3. Reed Avenue versus stormwater: compare the actual instruments

### Reed commitment and recorded approval

The [Reed ordinance packet, page 1](https://cheyenne.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?view_id=5&clip_id=1093&meta_id=147338#page=1)
commits **$5,000,000 from General Fund Reserves**, for planning, design,
right-of-way acquisition, construction and related costs. It contains two
important constraints:

- No expenditure from **this commitment** until a final BNSF agreement is reached.
- Unexpended, unobligated funds are to be uncommitted on June 30, 2027.

This unsigned packet is not itself an executed ordinance. The [April 27 minutes,
page 2](https://cheyenne.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?view_id=5&clip_id=1095&meta_id=147723#page=2)
record third/final approval as **Ordinance 4669**, with **Moody voting no**.
A clerk's note expressly says **no vote was recorded for Rinne due to technical
issues**. Do not turn the broader “all members except…” wording into a fabricated
Rinne YES vote or claim unanimity.

The earlier Ordinance 4662 reference is a different matter: the [March 23 minutes,
page 2](https://cheyenne.granicus.com/DocumentViewer.php?file=cheyenne_2a5c2428a6390c52750d45788dfeb157.pdf&view=1#page=2)
identify 4662 as repeal of a stormwater user-fee ordinance—not the Reed allocation.
Those March minutes cannot be used as evidence of an October vote.

April 27 page 5 also records a separate **$650,000 property purchase for Greenway
and Compliance use**, funded from 2021 SPOT and General Fund sources. Adding it
to $5 million arithmetically does not prove a single $5.65 million Reed reserve
appropriation. Transaction scope and funding joins must be established first.

### October 26 stormwater action

The [October 26 minutes, page 3](https://cheyenne.granicus.com/DocumentViewer.php?file=cheyenne_740ab3049f2e7e143ca88004317ff695.pdf&view=1#page=3)
record a motion to adopt a resolution amending General Fund Balance Policy for
**fiscal-year-end review and potential stormwater transfers up to an aggregate
$10 million**. The motion failed. **Aldrich and White are expressly named as YES**;
the minutes say the remaining governing-body members voted no.

This was not an executed $10 million transfer. Nor was it merely a failed floor
amendment with a separately approved main transfer motion: it was the failed
motion to adopt the policy-changing resolution. The supplied **7–2 tally is not
reproduced**; the page-1 roster lists the mayor and nine council members. This
report preserves the source wording rather than manufacturing an individual
roll-call dataset from the roster.

The Reed and stormwater actions differ in **date, instrument, purpose, conditions
and transaction stage**. Their amounts alone do not prove an unlawful preference,
a missing cash balance, or interchangeable available funds.
