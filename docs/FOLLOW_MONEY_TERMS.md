## 1. Resolution 6573: what the financing packet actually provides

Source: [October 26 agenda attachment 151507](https://cheyenne.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?view_id=5&clip_id=1144&meta_id=151507#page=1),
located through the published generated agenda's uniquely matching loan item,

The [October 26 minutes, page 3](https://cheyenne.granicus.com/DocumentViewer.php?file=cheyenne_740ab3049f2e7e143ca88004317ff695.pdf&view=1#page=3)

### Purpose, source and conditions

The memorandum describes temporary financing of voter-approved SPOT projects
before sufficient tax collections accumulate. The lending source is the
**One Percent Sales Tax Capital Projects Fund**; the recipient is the **Special
Purpose Option Sales Tax Capital Projects Fund**. Repayment is to come from SPOT
revenues attributable to each project. The packet says collections begin January
1, 2027; actual collections were not independently verified here.

The [operative provisions, PDF page 3](https://cheyenne.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?view_id=5&clip_id=1144&meta_id=151507#page=3)
specify:

1. **No more than one transfer per project**, on or near the anticipated start
   date, based on actual financing needs.
2. **No transfer if sufficient SPOT revenues or other available project funds,
   including interest, have already accumulated.**
3. Each transfer is capped by the principal amount for that Exhibit A line.
4. Interest accrues **only on the amount actually transferred, from its transfer
   date**—not on the full $18.063 million starting at resolution adoption.
5. The rate is to be fixed using the lending fund's investment earnings rate as
   of adoption. The Treasurer must document the rate/calculation in a separate
   dated written certification. **No numeric rate is supplied in this packet.**
6. Principal and accrued interest must be repaid from that project's SPOT
   revenues **within four years of the actual transfer**, with penalty-free
   prepayment permitted.

The packet explicitly intends to compensate the lending fund for foregone
investment earnings. Describing this as an established interest-free loan would
be incorrect. Absence of the separate certification from this retrieved packet
does not prove that the certification does not exist.

The body refers to the adoption date, while the Exhibit's rate note refers to
the date the resolution is executed. The certified final version and dated rate
certification are needed to confirm the operative date/rate—not an assumed rate.

### Cash-flow and interest questions that remain answerable

The packet reports **$83,356,656 in lending-fund cash and investments as of June
30, 2026** and says a cash-flow analysis supports financing availability. That is
an assertion in the packet—not an independently reconciled November cash balance,
proof that the full amount is unrestricted, or proof those funds could lawfully
be redirected to stormwater.

The rate rule also matters for loans anticipated to start as late as 2029:
request the certified adoption-date rate, assumptions and actual interest
postings. A fixed 2026 rate and later transfer date are documented design choices,
not by themselves proof of a subsidy or loss. Actual interest/foregone yield
cannot be calculated from the cap alone.

### What this changes—and does not change

- There is now a **documented contract-to-fund-family link** for #7782 in two
  budget schedules. It is no longer accurate to say there is no funding-history
  evidence for this contract.
- This supports treating Reed's older SPOT funding separately from the later
  conditional **$5 million General Fund reserve commitment**. It does not prove
  which fund paid any one invoice or how costs were split between 2012/2017.
- The schedules are **outstanding commitments, not lifetime spending totals,
  original contract ceilings, completed deliverables or bank-cleared payments**.
- Their difference is $79,891 at the schedules' printed precision. It may include
  payments, releases, cancellations, amendments or other adjustments. It must
  not be labeled “paid” without the PO/GL reconciliation.
- The six previously verified July 23, 2026 voucher entries total **$53,615.51**
  under the same PSA #7782. July is after the June 30 snapshot. Do not subtract
  those entries from $596,130 and announce a current outstanding balance: the
  actual postings, other activity and any adjustments are not yet reconciled.

**Still missing:** original contract/PO and amendments, vendor invoices and
service periods, the two years' PO activity, fund/project-coded GL postings and
payment confirmations. The BNSF packet exists in the archive as an image-only
extraction lead; it was not newly reviewed here. No final-execution date or
satisfaction of the reserve ordinance's BNSF condition is claimed.
