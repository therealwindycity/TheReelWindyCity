Does the city clerk certify the referendum — and does a judge let it stand?

Organizers turned in roughly 3,200 signatures; 2,739 valid Cheyenne-registered 2024 voters are required under the Title 22 referendum statute. The city attorney is simultaneously asking a district court whether annexations must be fought under W.S. 15-1-409 instead.
