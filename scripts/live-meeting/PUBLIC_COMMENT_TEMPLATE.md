# Public comment template: Cheyenne City Council, October 12, 2026

Keep it to the time limit the chair announces (usually about 3 minutes, roughly 350 to 400 words spoken). Speak in your own voice. Every factual claim should cite the agenda item, the document, and the page or line.

## Structure

1. **Identity (10 sec):** "My name is ___. I live at ___ in Cheyenne. I am speaking on Item __."
2. **Ask (15 sec):** One sentence. "I ask the Council to table / vote no on / amend Item __."
3. **Record contradictions (90 to 120 sec):** Quote the record, then quote the conflicting record.
   - "On [date], [official] said [quote]. Today's packet says [quote]. Which is correct?"
   - "The staff memo for Item __ says [quote]. Ordinance __ says [quote]."
4. **Specific harm (45 sec):** Name the group affected and the mechanism. Example: "Electric fencing under §9.12.080(B) has no voltage or joule limit."
5. **Specific questions (30 sec):** Two or three questions that need an answer on the record.
6. **Close (10 sec):** Thank the Council.

## Fact-check before you speak

- [ ] Each quote matches the official packet text, not a transcript. Transcripts are machine-generated.
- [ ] Each item number matches the posted agenda (Granicus, not the stale city PDFs).
- [ ] Each statute cite is checked on the Wyoming Legislature site (wyoleg.gov).
- [ ] You are not claiming a legal conclusion you can't support. Phrase these as questions to the Council or City Attorney.
- [ ] You have the public-comment sign-up rules (deadline, in-person vs. remote, written submission option) from the City Clerk.
